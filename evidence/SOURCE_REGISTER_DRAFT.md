## Duplicate and common-source review

- E01–E04 are separate URLs in the same community forum and discuss overlapping context-limit concerns. E02 includes a follow-up and links other requests; replies may refer to the same users or prior reports. Use them as dated examples of requests, not a respondent count or independent prevalence sample.
- E06–E08 are separate Codex issue URLs. E06 concerns CLI/TUI defaults; E07 reports a Desktop UI change and includes an OpenAI contributor response; E08 reports a later post-merge Windows UI regression and explicitly links earlier issues. Do not treat all three as independent evidence of current universal behavior.
- E09 and E10 are independent third-party tools with similar goals. Their inferred counts are not platform measurements. E13 is also third-party, but addresses a separate browser-extraction failure mode. E05 and the founder's separate export experience are distinct people/reports and must remain separate.
- E11 and E12 are official support documentation with different scopes: Free-plan context notices and account export procedures. They do not verify the reported root causes in E03–E05.

No source in E01–E14 supports deliberate billing manipulation, common root cause, user-caused fault, or an incident rate. E14 is usable only as an attributed screenshot-based report; direct retrieval and exact publication date remain unverified. E13 and E14 describe related browser capture risks, not two independently reproduced defects. The founder's ~2.4 GB export experience remains a first-person report; its historical supporting Library record was not available in this execution, so the public wording must stay explicitly attributed and causal claims must remain removed.
